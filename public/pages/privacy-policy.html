My Eyes and Ears

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how My Eyes and Ears ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password (stored in encrypted form), profile photo, and role (such as caregiver or parent).
  • Family and care information: family name, member details, relationships, medications, appointments, check-in reminders, contacts, accessibility preferences, and related notes.
  • Device and usage information: push notification tokens (FCM), app activity needed to deliver notifications and maintain account security.
  • Authentication data: information from email verification, password reset, or social login providers when you choose to use them.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user and family accounts.
  • Provide care coordination features within your family group.
  • Send reminders, alerts, and service-related notifications.
  • Verify accounts, prevent fraud, and protect the Service.
  • Improve performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests and legal obligations.

3. Family Data Sharing

Information you add about family members may be visible to authorized caregivers and members within the same family group, based on app permissions and roles. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Push Notifications

With your permission, we use device notification tokens to send check-in reminders, medication alerts, appointment updates, and other care-related notifications. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted passwords and secure server infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your account information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features such as notifications.
  • Request information about how your data is processed.

To make a privacy request, contact us at privacy@myeyesandears.com.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for caregivers managing family care. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without appropriate parental or guardian authorization.

9. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers for hosting, authentication, email delivery, analytics, or push notification delivery. These providers process data only as needed to perform services on our behalf.

10. International Users

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. By using the Service, you consent to such processing in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at privacy@myeyesandears.com.
